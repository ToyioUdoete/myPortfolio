WVAQ Workshop at WACV 2027

Restoring Ovarian Ultrasound for Reliable Follicle Detection

Toyiomfon Udoete and Shivanand Sheshappanavar

A diffusion-based restoration method for ovarian ultrasound, judged by whether follicle detection gets more reliable afterwards rather than by image scores alone. Posterior-sample fusion combines several restored samples into one detection. Trained and tested on a leak-free split, with an out-of-domain test on MMOTU.

FABRIC Workshop at WACV 2027

Trust but Verify: Training-Free Evidence Verification for VLM-Generated Ovarian Ultrasound Reports

Toyiomfon Udoete and Shivanand Sheshappanavar

Vision-language models like MedGemma can write fluent ultrasound reports that are not grounded in the image. Trust but Verify checks each statement against detected follicle evidence and rejects the claims it can't support, without retraining or changing the model.

WVAQ Workshop at WACV 2027

Frozen-Backbone Adaptation: An Empirical Study of Component Substitution in a Pretrained Deraining Network

Toyiomfon Udoete, Alisha Khan, Sudipa Roy, Judith Nkechinyere Njoku and Diksha Shukla

How much can you change a pretrained deraining network without retraining it? We keep the RAINet backbone frozen and swap in convolution, attention and multi-scale embedding components, then repeat the study on OneRestore to see which findings hold up.

About me

I'm from Nigeria, and I came to AI from software engineering and graphic design.

I'm finishing an MS in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Wyoming, advised by Dr. Shivanand Sheshappanavar. My research is on diffusion-based image restoration and on making vision-language models in medicine verifiable, mostly through ovarian ultrasound for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS).

Before research, I worked as a graphic designer and built websites, so I care about how results look and read, not only whether they work.

I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and looking for a research internship for Summer 2027.
